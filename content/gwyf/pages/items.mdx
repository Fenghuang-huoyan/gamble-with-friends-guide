## Three ways an item turns on

Most “this item does nothing” posts are an activation miss. Sort the shelf into three buckets before you memorize names.

**Shift actives.** The casino day has to be running. Time Machine is the strict example: one press, during the shift, never in the lobby, and it cannot rewind earlier than the start of the day. Taser has to touch a keypad. Golden Chip spends itself as one free all-in at whatever the cap is right then. Quota Gun has to be aimed at a body. Mystery Box still needs someone to pick up the result.

**Timed and nearby.** Holy Statue shields players standing by the holder, and only while it is active. Drink raises profit during a short drunk window and blurs vision. Microphone raises nearby profit while the holder sings. Camera banks a cut of someone else’s win if their head is in frame at that second, then you smash it to cash out. A loss on camera wipes the bank inside the camera.

**Passives you hold.** Stake Holder amplifies other items and, in V1.0.30-checked notes, scales with the quota so it is worth more later. It does not upgrade the Time Machine. Gambler's Confidence is described as a profit bump for the whole day. Insurance reduces losses; the exact moment it applies is fuzzier than the tooltip paraphrase, so that timing stays Unverified. Bonus Draw pays a ticket when you profit, which is only good if you can profit on repeat.

## What to buy on a normal day

With a handful of tickets, Golden Chip is the clean single purchase if someone is willing to pair it with a Taser later. The pair is the [combo page](/item-combos), and the order is the whole trick.

Holy Statue is the defensive buy when the crew can stand together and bet fast games for a few seconds. It is a bad buy if everyone wanders.

Skip Mystery Box until you have a plan. No developer has published odds. Community percentage lists are estimates. They are not reprinted here.

Skip cosmetics on a save you are trying to finish. Hats are account unlocks in community reports; the tickets are not. Spend run tickets on the next day’s item.

## Body parts are not an item combo

The store says you can sell a body part for tickets. The Quota Gun is the item that turns a part into quota progress, and only as an emergency while you are under the line. Shooting the person who is holding the Taser is how you solve today’s quota and delete tomorrow’s plan. Mouth and eye shots also have non-quota costs: guides mention blocked speech and a darkened eye. Those side effects are Unverified detail, and they are a reason to aim carefully anyway.
