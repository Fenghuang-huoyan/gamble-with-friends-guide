## What a reload actually does

The useful mental model, from the Steam guide’s Mine Sweeper demonstration and from later seed write-ups: a lot of tables deal their outcome when the day starts, not when you click. You play, you learn a bomb or a hand, you quit before the day is resolved, you load, and the same deal is waiting. You then play the information.

That is the opposite of “reload until the roulette ball is nice.” A tips page checked against V1.0.30 says card decks come back in the same order, and that physics tables do not. It names Plinko and the wheels as the ones the trick does not touch.

## What the sources actually sort

**Replays, with a source.** Mine Sweeper, in the Steam 100% guide, including a worked example of three failed boards becoming a correct one. Blackjack and 1P Poker, via the “card decks replay” tip. Duck Race and Dragon Tower, via save-scum guides that tell you to memorize a winner or a path.

**Does not replay, with a source.** Plinko. Wheel of Fortune and the Money Wheel, grouped as “the wheels.” Clean Fall is a 24x on that physics board. The bat throw (Q, middle pin, third row) is the assist players use instead of a reload. It is not certain.

**Not sorted by this site.** Crash, Penguin Cross, Keno, HiLo, Street Craps, Slots, Roulette, Baccarat. Roulette is a physical ball in the fiction and might belong with the wheels. It might not. The games table marks those Unverified. A minimum-bet test is the check, and it takes one day, not a theory.

## Using it for achievements, quota, and endings

Keep the three jobs apart.

- **Achievements.** Accept the Phone Booth challenge first. Then reload the table that challenge names, if that table replays. A perfect replay on the wrong challenge still does not unlock.
- **Quota.** Use a reload to test one recovery route. Do not save-scum five machines while the real clock runs down. If the timer is on, the test itself costs minutes unless you quit and reload the whole day.
- **Endings.** The coin is the important seed. The [endings page](/endings) has the four-step Esc route. The short version: payoff first, quit at the popup, take one coin, quit again, move the shop, take the other coin.

## What this is not

It is not a trainer, a file editor, or the developer console. V1.0.30 did add a console on the backtick key for spawning and teleporting. This page does not document those commands. Save scum here means quit and load, which the game already lets you do, plus the honesty to stop when a table will not repeat.
