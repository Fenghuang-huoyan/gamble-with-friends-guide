## One table, not seventeen articles

You do not need a thin page per machine. You need to know whether the thing in front of you can wipe the shared bank, whether a reload will teach you anything, and which Phone Booth challenge it can satisfy. Filter the list. Risk words (lower, medium, higher) are this site’s reading of how fast a shared bank dies in community write-ups. They are not drop rates and they are not official.

## How to read a floor

Floor 1 is the small set: Roulette, Blackjack, Slots, Street Craps, Duck Race, Wheel of Fortune. Floor 2 is where Crash, Plinko, Penguin Cross, Keno, HiLo, and the Money Wheel show up in guides. Floor 3 adds 1P Poker, Dragon Tower, and Mine Sweeper. Floor 4 is described as harder versions of tables you have met, not as a secret new game.

The store says each visit randomizes the games. A guide’s “debut” list is the pool, not a guarantee that every machine is placed today. If the challenge wants Plinko and Plinko did not spawn, reroll the day rather than forcing a different table and blaming the achievement.

## Reload, in one paragraph

The Steam 100% guide shows Mine Sweeper bombs repeating after a quit. Card hands are described the same way. Duck Race winners and Dragon Tower paths are the other community memory tricks. Plinko is a physics board, and a tips page checked against V1.0.30 says the wheels do not replay either. For everything marked unverified in the Reload column, do not bet the bank on a pattern you have not tested yourself. The method, including how a shop action moves a stuck result, is on the [save scum page](/save-scum).

## A quota day versus an achievement day

On a quota day, pick the lower-variance thing that is actually in the room and stop when the timer-watcher says the debt for today is covered. On an achievement day, accept the booth challenge first, then look for that one table. Mixing both in the same five minutes is how Lucky Streak eats the quota.

Blackjack’s “stand on 16” style advice in general guides is ordinary card sense, not a measured house edge for this game. This page does not invent payout tables the developer did not publish. V1.0.30 did say Crash and Baccarat payouts were tweaked, so any launch-week payout chart is stale even where it was accurate.
