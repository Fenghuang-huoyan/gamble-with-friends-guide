## Spend run tickets on the run

The store is blunt: tickets buy cosmetics in the neighborhood, it is not optimal, and your friends may scream. On a save you care about, that scream is correct. A hat does not raise a keypad cap. A Golden Chip might.

Do the fashion pass on a save you are willing to throw away, after you understand the loop below is a community report.

## What both reports share

GameRant and a later accessory write-up describe the same economy glitch, in different button orders:

- Unlock state for clothes is account-wide, not trapped in the save you bought them on.
- Starting funds come back when you make a new save.
- Therefore a throwaway save can buy the current stock, and a newer throwaway save can buy the next stock, without spending the tickets from a serious run.

A separate V1.0.30-checked tip says tickets belong to the save, not the profile, and a new game puts you back on a small float. That supports “funds reset.” It does not, by itself, prove unlocks persist. The account-wide claim is the GameRant sentence. Unverified, and plausible, and not something to bet a 100% file on.

Stock is also described as random and as changing after each in-game day. If that is still true, simply playing days rotates the mannequins. The reset loop is the impatient version.

## What is confirmed about cosmetics

V1.0.11 added more cosmetics and adjusted meshes so they fit the body. V1.0.30 added another batch and said previous ones got fixes. So the shop is a live part of the game, and older “every item” lists are incomplete. This page does not inventory every hat. The useful question is whether the unlock survived a deleted save. Watch the mannequin on the next file. If the owned piece is gone, the account-wide claim was wrong on your build, and the loop stops being worth it.

Emotes and customization are a store bullet (“many emotes + customization options”). How emotes are bound was not documented in the sources used here.

## If you only want one outfit

Play a day, clear quota, buy the single piece, and leave the rest of the tickets for items. That path uses only the store’s own description. It is slower. It is also the one that cannot wreck a save file.
