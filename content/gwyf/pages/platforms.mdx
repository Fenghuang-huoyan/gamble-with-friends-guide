## What a Windows PC needs

Minimum, from the store: Windows 10, Core i5-6600, 8 GB RAM, GTX 970, DirectX 11, broadband, 3 GB free.

Recommended: Windows 11, Core i7-8700, 8 GB RAM, GTX 1070, DirectX 12, the same network and disk.

Those are the developer’s numbers. This page does not add a frame-rate claim. V1.0.30 added input mapping, a higher controller-sensitivity cap, and accessibility options that already existed for head bob and camera smoothing (the FAQ points at Settings, Accessibility). Motion-sick players are told to avoid the body shredder. That is official advice, and it is more useful than a fake benchmark.

The US price observed on 18 Oct 2026 was $7.99, discounted to $4.95. It will move. The store page lists Family Sharing as a feature.
