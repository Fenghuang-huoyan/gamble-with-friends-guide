## Are achievements bugged right now?

People report feats that do not pop. A lot of those reports are the booth. Separate from that, older community pages described a set of glitched achievements, sometimes “about nine,” tied to builds around v1.0.4. Later notes say some of those were fixed. This page was checked on 18 Oct 2026 and did **not** re-test a glitch list on a live client.

So: no achievement below is marked glitched. If you want a bug report, use Pause Menu, Report A Bug, or support@tenstack.se, and say which challenge was accepted. Do not trust a recycled v1.0.4 list.

## Clean Fall, because reload will not save you

Clean Fall is the Floor 2 Plinko 24x. Plinko is physics. Quitting and reloading does not deal you the same drop the way Mine Sweeper deals you the same bombs.

The community assist: baseball bats are lying around, including near the entrance. Carry one to the board, tap Q to throw it lightly, and aim at the middle pin on the third row so the bat blocks the inner lanes and sheds balls toward the outside cups. Guides that claim a V1.0.30 check say the bat was still there. It raises the chance. It does not guarantee the cup. And it still does nothing if the Phone Booth challenge was not Clean Fall.

## Money achievements are a running total

Steam’s internal ids are ACH_MONEY_10M, 100M, 1B, 10B, and 100B. Community names, in that order, are HARDER, BETTER, FASTER, STRONGER, and WORK IS NEVER OVER! The dollar figures follow the ids. The display names are the guide mapping. Minimum-quota days will not get you the last of these. You have to keep money in the run.

V1.0.10 added support for very large displayed numbers, and V1.0.30 said the old 5Qi cap was lifted. That is official. It does not change the five thresholds above.

## What is not an ending

The launch prompt is three achievements of its own. Yes and a win is The Gambler's Blessing. Yes and a loss is Unlucky :(, and guides say the game closes. No is Not Feeling It. The prompt returns, so you can collect the set across restarts. You Reached...PEAK! is the neighborhood obstacle course and the BANG BANG pickup. Real Gambling is not Cool is the GET HELP menu. None of those five are day-13 endings.

The three hidden ending names on the store page are Let It Ride!, End of the Line, and ...And They Lived Happily After. Their route, including the Esc timing, is on the [endings page](/endings).

## A sane 100% order

1. Boot prompt and the help menu, whenever. They are not tied to the booth.
2. Floor 1 challenges before you care about Floor 2. One a day.
3. Same pattern on Floors 2, 3, and 4. Deliberate losses only after quota.
4. Money totals fall out of a run that keeps playing past the minimum. Do not start a fresh save just to “save” money. Tickets do not carry between saves; the money achievements are on the profile as Steam unlocks, but the cash itself is in the save.
5. Endings last, on a save that is not Endless, with the [one-save Esc route](/endings).
6. Anything that still fails: booth checklist again, then a bug report. Not a downloaded unlocker.
